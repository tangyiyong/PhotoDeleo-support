照片去重隐私政策

更新日期 2026 年 11 月 12 日

设备上的数据

照片去重使用你授权访问的照片、拍摄日期和照片已有的位置来扫描及浏览图库。照片、匹配特征、位置和图库索引在设备上处理，应用不会把这些数据发送给开发者。应用没有广告或追踪 SDK。

本地索引与权限

扫描索引用于续扫。开启人物功能后，可为用户选择的照片创建本地人脸索引。你可以在应用设置中清除扫描索引、关闭人物功能并清除人脸索引，也可在系统设置中调整照片权限。

Apple 服务

你允许获取 iCloud 原件时，Apple 可按你的 iCloud 设置下载照片。购买、兑换和恢复由 Apple StoreKit 处理。启用 iCloud 照片时，系统删除可能同步到其他设备。

反馈和本网站

开发者会收到你主动提交的反馈。GitHub 上的支持议题公开，TestFlight 反馈由 Apple 处理并提供给开发者。本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，站点未加入分析或广告脚本。有关 GitHub 对访问数据的处理，请查阅 GitHub 隐私声明。隐私问题可通过支持页联系。

PhotoDeleo Privacy Policy

Updated November 12, 2026

On-device data

PhotoDeleo uses photos you allow it to access, capture dates, and locations already in your photos to scan and browse your library. It processes photos, match features, locations, and library indexes on your device and does not send them to the developer. The app uses no advertising or tracking SDKs.

Local indexes and permissions

The scan index supports resuming. If you enable People, a local face index can be created for photos you choose. In app Settings, you can clear the scan index, turn off People, and clear the face index. You can also change Photos access in system settings.

Apple services

If you allow iCloud originals, Apple can retrieve the originals under your iCloud settings. Apple StoreKit handles purchases, redemptions, and restores. If iCloud Photos is enabled, Photos deletions can sync to other devices.

Feedback and this website

The developer receives feedback you choose to submit. GitHub support issues are public. Apple handles TestFlight feedback and makes it available to the developer. GitHub Pages hosts this site, which includes no analytics or advertising scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of website visits. For privacy questions, use the support page.
