照片去重支持

找不到照片或扫描暂停

先检查系统照片访问权限和本次扫描范围。部分授权只覆盖所选照片。如果原件在 iCloud，可按需允许系统获取原件后重试。设备热状态达到临界时，等待系统热状态回落后继续。

照片预览与人物扫描

打开图库里的照片后，左右滑动可切换照片。双指捏合可放大，双击可复位。若高清图仅在 iCloud，可在预览中主动请求加载。打开「图库 → 人物」可启动本地人脸检测，检查数量和读取失败会显示在扫描控件下方。

恢复购买

在设置中选择「恢复购买」，使用原购买所用的 Apple 账户。

取消清理或查找已删除的照片

在清单中取消勾选，或在系统确认时取消。提交后可在「照片」的「最近删除」中查看是否仍可恢复。启用 iCloud 照片时，删除可能同步到其他设备。

提交反馈

TestFlight 用户可在 TestFlight 中选择「发送 Beta 反馈」。也可在 GitHub 提交支持请求。请附应用版本、系统版本和复现步骤。GitHub 议题公开，请勿附私人照片、人脸截图或账户资料。

PhotoDeleo Support

Missing photos or a paused scan

Check Photos access in system settings and the selected scan scope. Limited access covers only selected photos. If originals are in iCloud, allow retrieval when needed and try again. If the system reports a critical thermal state, resume after it cools.

Photo previews and face detection

Open a library photo and swipe left or right to browse. Pinch to zoom and double-tap to reset. If the full preview is only in iCloud, request it from the preview screen. Go to Library → People to start on-device face detection and see how many photos were checked or could not be read.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings, choose Restore Purchases, and use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Cancel cleanup or find deleted photos

Clear the selection or cancel at the system confirmation. After deletion, check Photos’ Recently Deleted album to see if the items are still recoverable. With iCloud Photos enabled, deletions can sync to other devices.

Send feedback

For support or privacy questions, email tangyiyong@qq.com.

In TestFlight, choose Send Beta Feedback. You can also open a GitHub support issue. Include your app version, OS version, and steps to reproduce. GitHub issues are public. Keep personal photos, face screenshots, and account details out of reports.
